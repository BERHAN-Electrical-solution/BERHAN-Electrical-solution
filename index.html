Who we are

About Berhan Electrical Solutions

Berhan Electrical Solutions is a technical services company based in Kera Kore, Addis Ababa. We provide practical, dependable installations and repairs for homes and businesses across the city.

Our work covers four core areas — electrical, TV/satellite, CCTV & intercom, and internet/network — so customers can rely on one team for connected, secure, well‑wired spaces.

General Electrical Work TV / Satellite Installation CCTV & Intercom Internet / Network

What we stand for

  • Quality, safety and attention to detail on every job.
  • Clean, organised installation and cable routing.
  • Solutions matched to the actual project and budget.
  • One company for electrical, security and connectivity.
